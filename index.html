How the body is organised

Every structure belongs to a level. Small changes at one level show up at the next.

  1. Cells — the smallest living unit, around 200 specialised types.
  2. Tissues — groups of similar cells: epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous.
  3. Organs — two or more tissues combined for a task, like the stomach.
  4. Systems — organs cooperating, like the digestive tract.
  5. Organism — all systems together: you.
